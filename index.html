Building an Asia-Pacific Community · For Shared Prosperity

2026 年，中国担任亚太经合组织（APEC）东道主。全年围绕「建设亚太共同体，促进共同繁荣」主题， 以「开放 · 创新 · 合作」三大优先领域为主线，推动亚太地区在开放中发展、在创新中跃升、在合作中共赢。

In 2026, China serves as APEC host. Guided by the theme “Building an Asia-Pacific Community for Shared Prosperity” and three priorities — Openness, Innovation and Cooperation — the year’s agenda advances open development, innovation-led growth and win-win cooperation across the region.

三大优先领域

开放 · 创新 · 合作 Openness · Innovation · Cooperation

以开放促联通、以创新促增长、以合作促共赢，三条主线彼此支撑，构成本年度 APEC 合作议程的总体框架。

Openness drives connectivity, innovation drives growth, and cooperation delivers shared gains. The three mutually reinforcing priorities define the overall framework of APEC’s 2026 cooperation agenda.

01

开放

Openness

Openness · 开放

坚持开放的区域主义与多边贸易体制，推动贸易投资自由化便利化，维护区域产供链开放、稳定、畅通。

Upholding open regionalism and the multilateral trading system; advancing trade and investment liberalisation and facilitation; keeping regional supply chains open, stable and unimpeded.

  • 区域互联互通与制度型开放
  • 亚太自贸区议程的持续推进
  • 贸易便利化与营商环境改善
  • Regional connectivity and institutional opening-up
  • Continued advancement of the FTAAP agenda
  • Trade facilitation and business environment reform
02

创新

Innovation

Innovation · 创新

把握数字经济和人工智能机遇，推动绿色低碳转型与中小企业赋能，培育亚太增长的新动能与新优势。

Seizing opportunities in the digital economy and artificial intelligence; advancing the green, low-carbon transition and empowering SMEs to foster new drivers of Asia-Pacific growth.

  • 数字经济与人工智能治理合作
  • 绿色低碳转型与能源转型
  • 中小企业与人力资源开发
  • Digital economy and AI governance cooperation
  • Green, low-carbon and energy transition
  • SME development and human resource capacity
03

合作

Cooperation

Cooperation · 合作

深化成员间政策协调与能力建设，促进包容发展与互联互通，让增长成果更公平地惠及各方人民。

Deepening policy coordination and capacity building among members; promoting inclusive development and connectivity so that the fruits of growth are shared more equitably.

  • 宏观经济政策对话与协调
  • 能力建设与经济技术合作
  • 包容发展：妇女、青年与弱势群体
  • Macroeconomic policy dialogue and coordination
  • Capacity building and economic & technical cooperation
  • Inclusive growth: women, youth and vulnerable groups
主办城市 · 深圳

深圳：中国改革开放的窗口 Shenzhen: A Window onto China’s Reform and Opening Up

深圳位于中国南部沿海、珠江口东岸，毗邻香港，是中国设立的首批经济特区之一，也是粤港澳大湾区的核心引擎城市。 从「三天一层楼」的建设速度到「创新之城」的产业跃迁，深圳本身就是亚太开放合作与发展活力的一个缩影。

Located on China’s southern coast on the eastern bank of the Pearl River Estuary and adjacent to Hong Kong, Shenzhen is one of China’s first special economic zones and a core engine of the Guangdong–Hong Kong–Macao Greater Bay Area. From its legendary construction speed to its rise as a city of innovation, Shenzhen itself mirrors the openness and dynamism of the Asia-Pacific.

地理位置

中国南部沿海、珠江口东岸，毗邻香港特别行政区。 Southern coast of China, eastern bank of the Pearl River Estuary, adjacent to the HKSAR.

城市定位

经济特区、全国性经济中心城市、粤港澳大湾区核心引擎、中国特色社会主义先行示范区。 Special Economic Zone, national economic centre, core engine of the Greater Bay Area, pilot demonstration zone.

产业特色

高新技术产业、先进制造、现代金融与海洋经济协同发展，创新创业氛围浓厚。 High-tech industries, advanced manufacturing, modern finance and the maritime economy.

开放气质

改革开放前沿与国际交往门户，海陆空铁综合交通枢纽，具备承办大型国际会议的成熟条件。 Frontier of reform and opening-up, international gateway, integrated transport hub.
